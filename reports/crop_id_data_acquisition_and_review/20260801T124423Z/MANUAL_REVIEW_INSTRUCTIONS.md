# Manual Review Instructions

Open the local HTML files under `datasets/crop_identity_v2/review_packets/`. Review every image against its source, crop identity, privacy, quality, derivative status and duplicate family. Record one allowed decision in the CSV; do not edit hashes or paths. The HTML form is intentionally non-persisting—transfer decisions to `candidate_review_manifest.csv` under version control review.

For recommendation packets, inspect every exact source page, confirm authority and crop/disease mapping, then separately approve English facts and Tamil meaning. Never approve chemical content without current official registration, dosage, restriction and crop-stage verification. `VERIFY AGAINST OFFICIAL SOURCE`.
