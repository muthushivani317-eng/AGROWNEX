Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · App: AgrowNex (Android)

1. Who we are

AgrowNex is a farming assistant app for Tamil Nadu farmers, operated by Maria Joseph Saron Fdo E (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact: agrowai.tn@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: mobile number (sign-in by OTP through Firebase).
  • Profile: name, language, district, block, village, landmark, years of farming, land holding, farmer type, optional photo, gender and education (optional).
  • Location: your device GPS position when you choose "use my location" during setup, and the farm boundaries you draw. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Farm records: crops, farm products, expected harvest, tasks, diary entries.
  • Photos and voice: crop/leaf photos you take for disease checks, and voice notes you record.
  • AI chat: questions you ask the assistant and its answers.
  • Technical: an app-install ID, device model, app version and crash/error reports.

We do not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, or payment card details.

3. Why we use it

  • Weather, market prices, crop calendar and daily tasks for your village and crops.
  • Crop disease detection from your photos, and answers in the AI chat.
  • Connecting you to an FPO or KVK — only if you ask for it.
  • Keeping your account secure and fixing app errors.

We do not sell your data and do not show advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase — phone sign-in and app crash reports (Crashlytics).
  • OpenAI and Google Gemini — process your crop photos, voice and chat questions to give answers.
  • Cloudflare R2 — stores photos and voice notes.
  • Railway — hosts our servers and database.
  • Sentry — server error reports (no request bodies, tokens or phone numbers).
  • FPO / KVK / buyers — your profile or produce details, only after you give consent in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time.

These providers may store data outside India, including in the United States. Market prices and weather are read from public sources (Agmarknet, data.gov.in, Open-Meteo); none of your data is sent to them.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location — fill your village and farm location.
  • Camera / photos — crop disease photos and profile photo.
  • Microphone — voice questions and voice notes.

You can refuse any permission; only that feature stops working.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you close your account, sign-in stops at once and your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. See Delete your account for what is deleted and the few records kept by law.

7. Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app (Profile → Edit profile), withdraw consent for sharing, and close your account (Profile → Account settings → Close account). You can also email us to access, correct or erase your data. We reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Photos are kept in private storage. Access by our staff is limited and logged.

9. Children

AgrowNex is meant for adult farmers (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.
