# Farm Inputs Marketplace — production gate

The v1 implementation records cash-on-delivery orders only. It does not collect
online payments and does not generate tax invoices.

Production launch is blocked until an Indian lawyer/chartered accountant confirms:

- AgrowAI/Ferido's seller-of-record registration and invoicing obligations;
- the applicable GST turnover threshold and whether any supplied categories
  require earlier registration or category-specific compliance;
- required invoice fields, tax rates/HSN classification, books and cash-receipt
  retention; and
- licensing, labelling and sale restrictions for each seed, fertilizer and
  pesticide product placed in the catalog.

Turnover must be treated as gross sales receipts for release-review purposes,
not profit. No earlier informal “₹40 lakh profit” assumption authorizes launch.
