Rice tungro disease (RTD) occurring in severe forms in the South East Asian
countries, is caused by Rice tungro bacilliform virus (RTBV) with a DNA genome
and Rice tungro spherical virus (RTSV) with an RNA genome. RTBV , capable of
inducing most of the symptoms of RTD, depends on RTSV for transmission by the
infected rice plants. Four strains of RTBV G1, G2, Ic and L could be differentiated
